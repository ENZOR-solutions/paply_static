What you can do in the app

Invoices on the go

Create, confirm, email and share invoices, issue credit notes and duplicate past invoices.

Core and up

Scan bills with the camera

The phone's document scanner straightens the page and AI reads the supplier, amounts and line items.

Core and up

Tap to Pay and payment links

Accept contactless cards on your phone or show a QR code, through your own Stripe account, where available.

Fiscalize from the app

Fiscalize invoices with FURS right from your phone. Card-paid invoices are fiscalized automatically.

Push notifications

Know straight away when an invoice arrives, is paid or needs your approval.

Approve and comment

Approve supplier bills, record payments and talk to your team with @mentions.

Switch between companies

Running more than one company? Switch with a tap.

Face and fingerprint unlock

Lock the app with biometrics and two-factor authentication. You decide how quickly it locks again.

Four languages

English, Slovenian, Croatian and Serbian, in light or dark mode.
