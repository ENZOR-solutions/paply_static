E-invoices from 2028

E-invoicing and fiscal verification in Slovenia

Slovenian businesses already verify cash and card invoices with FURS. From 1 January 2028, invoices between businesses must also be structured e-invoices. Here is how to be ready.

On this page

The short version Timeline Fiscal verification (FURS) E-invoices from 2028 How Paply helps FAQ

The short version

  • Today: invoices paid in cash, which in Slovenia also includes cards, must be verified with the Financial Administration (FURS) as they are issued.
  • Public sector: invoices to public bodies have been sent as e-invoices for years.
  • From 1 January 2028: invoices between businesses must be issued and received as structured e-invoices.

Timeline

Today

Fiscal verification of cash and card invoices

Every invoice paid in cash or by card is sent to FURS and carries the ZOI and EOR codes and a QR code.

1 January 2028

Mandatory B2B e-invoices

Businesses exchange invoices with each other as structured e-invoices based on EN 16931, in the eSLOG 2.0 format.

Fiscal verification (FURS)

Before you can fiscalize, you register your business premises and electronic devices with FURS. When you issue an invoice, a protective mark (ZOI) is calculated and the invoice is sent to FURS, which returns a unique identifier (EOR). Both codes and a QR code appear on the invoice.

If FURS can't be reached, the invoice is still issued with its ZOI and must be submitted again later. Paply does that resubmission for you automatically.

E-invoices from 2028

From 1 January 2028 a PDF by email will no longer be enough between businesses. Invoices will travel as XML in the Slovenian eSLOG 2.0 format, aligned with the European standard EN 16931, so they go straight into your customer's accounting system.

The good news: if your invoices already live in a proper invoicing tool, the switch is mostly invisible. You keep filling in the same form; only the delivery changes.

FAQ

Do I need fiscal verification if my customers pay by bank transfer?

Fiscal verification applies to invoices paid in cash, which in Slovenia includes payment cards and similar methods. Invoices paid only by bank transfer are generally not verified with FURS. Check your specific case with your accountant.

When do e-invoices become mandatory between businesses?

From 1 January 2028. Until then, you can keep sending invoices to businesses by email with a PDF.

Can Paply register my business premises with FURS?

Yes. You add your premises and devices in Paply's settings, upload your FURS certificate, and Paply registers them and fiscalizes your invoices from then on.

Is Paply available in Slovenian?

Yes. Paply is developed in Slovenia, and the app, invoices and customer emails are all available in Slovenian.
