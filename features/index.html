Fiscalization and compliance

The legal side of invoicing in Slovenia and Croatia, handled as part of issuing an invoice.

FURS fiscalization (Slovenia)

ZOI and EOR codes, business premises and devices, a verification QR code on the invoice and automatic resubmission if FURS is unreachable.

Fiscalization in Croatia

ZKI and JIR from the Tax Administration, a verification QR code on the invoice and a recovery path when the service is down.

Statutory archive

Issued and received invoices are kept for the legal retention period: 10 years in Slovenia and 11 years in Croatia.

Correct VAT breakdown

VAT is grouped by rate the way the tax rules require, and KPD classification codes can be added for Croatian invoices.

VAT threshold watch

For Slovenian companies outside the VAT system, Paply warns you when your turnover approaches the registration threshold.
